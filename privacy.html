Privacy Policy

Square Connector · Clear Ledger LLC · Effective October 27, 2026

Square Connector is a desktop tool that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what data it uses and where that data goes.

Data Square Connector uses

  • Square export files you choose to load (sales, tenders, tips, fees, and deposit details).
  • QuickBooks Online data needed to post entries: your company name, your chart of accounts, and journal entry numbers (to avoid posting the same entry twice).
  • Connection credentials: the app's Intuit client credentials and the OAuth tokens Intuit issues when you connect.

Where it is stored

All of this stays on the computer running Square Connector. Settings, history, tokens, and error logs are saved as files in the Square Connector folder. Clear Ledger does not operate a server that receives your data, and Square Connector does not send your data to Clear Ledger or to any third party other than Intuit.

How it is shared

Square Connector sends journal entries only to the QuickBooks Online company you connect, using Intuit's API over encrypted HTTPS. During sign-in, Intuit redirects your browser through a page hosted on GitHub Pages that forwards the sign-in response to Square Connector on your computer; that page does not store or transmit the response anywhere else.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or analytics.
  • Your QuickBooks data is not shown to anyone other than you.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your computer until you delete it. Disconnecting in Square Connector revokes its QuickBooks access with Intuit. Deleting the Square Connector folder removes all stored settings, history, tokens, and logs.

Security

Credentials are kept in local files on your computer and are never shown in the browser or written to browser logs. Protect that computer with a password and keep its operating system up to date.
