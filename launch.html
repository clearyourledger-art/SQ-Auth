Square Connector

A Clear Ledger LLC product

Square Connector turns Square sales exports into QuickBooks Online journal entries and posts them to your company.

It runs on your own computer. To open it, start Square Connector (double-click START (Windows).bat in the Square Connector folder), then continue below.

Open Square Connector

To connect QuickBooks, go to Account Map & Settings → Connect to QuickBooks inside Square Connector.
